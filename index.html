Where Australia Plays.

An exploration of where public playgrounds are located, how their characteristics differ, and what makes each play space unique.

THE GEOGRAPHY OF PLAY

Where are Australia's playgrounds?

Explore our study regions and discover how playgrounds are distributed across Manningham.

Where Are Our Study Regions?

This project explores public playgrounds across selected communities in southeastern Australia, drawing on datasets from local councils and government agencies. These regions provide opportunities to investigate playground distribution, classification, size, shade and available facilities. The datasets differ in geographic coverage and recorded attributes. Rather than treating them as a complete picture of Australian playgrounds, we use regional case studies to explore the characteristics of public play spaces.

Where Are Manningham's Different Playground Types?

Playgrounds in Manningham are not evenly distributed. The mapped locations are concentrated in the western part of the city, while relatively few appear in the east. Small playgrounds appear particularly common, with larger classifications scattered across the region. The map shows recorded playground locations and classifications, rather than measuring how accessible playgrounds are to residents.

SHADE & PLAYGROUND FEATURES

A place in the shade.

Shade is an important characteristic of outdoor play spaces. Explore how shade classifications vary across Canberra's playgrounds and whether they differ with playground size.

How Shaded Are Canberra's Playgrounds?

More than half of active ACT playgrounds are classified as having FAIR shade. GOOD is the next most common classification, while smaller proportions are recorded as POOR or NO SHADE. Missing shade information is shown separately.

How Does Shade Provision Vary with Playground Size?

This heatmap compares shade classifications across different playground size ranges in Canberra. Darker cells indicate a higher percentage of playgrounds within that size range, allowing shade provision to be compared regardless of how many playgrounds each group contains.
