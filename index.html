Australian Public Playgrounds

Where Are Our Study Regions?

This project explores public playgrounds across selected communities in southeastern Australia, drawing on datasets from local councils and government agencies. These regions provide opportunities to investigate playground distribution, classification, size, shade and available facilities. The datasets differ in geographic coverage and recorded attributes. Rather than treating them as a complete picture of Australian playgrounds, we use regional case studies to explore the characteristics of public play spaces.

Where Are Manningham's Different Playground Types?

Playgrounds in Manningham are not evenly distributed. The mapped locations are concentrated in the western part of the municipality, while relatively few appear in the east. Small playgrounds appear particularly common, with larger classifications scattered across the region. The map shows recorded playground locations and classifications, rather than measuring how accessible playgrounds are to residents.
